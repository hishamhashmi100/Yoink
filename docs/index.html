How it works

You never have to copy a link.

  1. Tap ShareUnder any video in the YouTube app, or on youtube.com in your browser.
  2. Tap YoinkIf it isn't in the first row, tap More and find Yoink in the list. After the first time, Yoink usually shows up right away.
  3. Pick a qualityA small sheet opens over YouTube. Tap the quality you want, or Audio (MP3).
  4. You're free to goNo need to keep YouTube open or keep watching. Close it, lock your phone, use other apps. Yoink downloads in the background, tells you when it's done, and the video waits in its Library.

What it does

A free YouTube downloader for Android. It saves videos, or just the audio as MP3, to your phone, and stays out of your way.

  • Any quality you want

    Best, 1080p, 720p or 480p, or just the sound as an MP3. One video, or a whole playlist in one go.

  • Runs in the background

    Close YouTube or lock your phone and it keeps downloading. A notification shows the speed and how long is left.

  • Library and player

    Search and play everything you saved. Videos loop if you like, and MP3s keep playing in the background.

  • Looks how you like

    Any color, glass or frosted glass, pure black, and 18 fonts. Save your looks as themes.

  • Fixes itself

    The downloader updates once a day, so YouTube changes rarely break it for long.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no ads, no tracking. It all happens on your phone.

How to install

Takes about a minute. Yoink isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Tap “Download for Android” If your browser asks whether to keep the file, tap Download or Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file Tap Open in the notification, or find yoink in your Files → Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installing from your browser Android will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
    You only need to do this once. You can turn it off again after installing.
  4. Tap Install If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. It warns about every app that doesn't come from the Play Store.
  5. Open Yoink once Allow notifications so you can see download progress. That's it.

Questions

The ones people ask most.

Is it safe?

Yoink is open source: anyone can read every line of the code on GitHub. The APK is built and signed automatically by GitHub from that public code, and each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can confirm your file wasn't changed.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Google Play doesn't allow apps that download from YouTube, so Yoink is distributed here instead, the same way many open-source Android apps are.

Does it need a server or an account?

No. Everything happens on your phone. There's no sign-in, no tracking and no ads.

Downloads stopped working. What do I do?

YouTube changes things often. Yoink updates its downloader by itself once a day, so it usually fixes itself. To fix it right away, open Yoink → Settings → Update downloader. If that doesn't help, check Settings → App updates for a newer version of Yoink.

Can I download a whole playlist?

Yes. Share the playlist to Yoink (or a video from it and tap Download the whole playlist), pick a quality or MP3, and every video downloads, two at a time, with one notification for the lot. There's no limit on how many videos, but longer playlists take a little longer to start, while Yoink reads the list of videos.

Where do my videos go?

Into Yoink's Library. In Settings you can also choose to save them to your gallery (Movies/Yoink and Music/Yoink).

How do I update Yoink?

Yoink updates itself. It checks for a new version once a day, and when one is out you'll see an Update card on the home screen and a notification. Tap Update and confirm in Android's installer. You can also check any time in Settings → App updates. Your library and settings are kept.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Apple doesn't allow apps like this on iPhone or iPad, so Yoink is Android only.
